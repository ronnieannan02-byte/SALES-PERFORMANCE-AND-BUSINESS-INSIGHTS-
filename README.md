This dataset contains e-commerce sales transactions from 2022 to 2026, covering various product categories, customer segments, payment methods, and regions across India. It is designed for data analysis, business intelligence, and machine learning projects.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data to uncover sales trends, product performance, regional performance, customer purchasing behaviour, and payment preferences. The analysis uses Microsoft Excel PivotTables, charts, and an interactive dashboard to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains *1,500 sales transactions* across multiple products, product categories, regions, customer types, and payment methods.

The data covers transactions from *2022 to 2026* and includes information on order dates, quantities, prices, and total sales.

## Dataset Fields

| Column         | Description                                  |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Order_ID       | Unique reference assigned to an order        |
| Order_Date     | Date the transaction occurred                |
| Year           | Year of the transaction                      |
| Month          | Month of the transaction                     |
| Quarter        | Quarter in which the transaction occurred    |
| Product_Name   | Product purchased by the customer            |
| Category       | Product category                             |
| Region         | Location where the sale was recorded         |
| Customer_Type  | Customer segment: New, Returning, or Premium |
| Payment_Method | Method used to pay for the order             |
| Quantity       | Number of units purchased                    |
| Unit_Price     | Selling price per unit                       |
| Total_Sales    | Total value of the transaction               |

## Business Questions

The project seeks to answer the following questions:

1. *Which products contribute the most to total sales?*
2. *How does sales performance vary across months and years?*
3. *Which regions generate the highest sales revenue?*
4. *How do New, Returning, and Premium customers compare in terms of sales revenue?*
5. *Which payment methods are most frequently used, and how much revenue does each generate?*

## Key Analysis Areas

### Product Performance

Products are compared based on their contribution to total revenue to identify the strongest and weakest-performing products.

### Sales Trends

Monthly and yearly sales are analyzed to identify changes in sales performance and periods of higher or lower revenue.

### Regional Performance

Sales across *Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Pune* are compared to identify the markets generating the most revenue.

### Customer Segmentation

Revenue generated by *New, Returning, and Premium customers* is compared to understand the contribution of each customer group.

### Payment Analysis

Payment methods including *Card, Cash on Delivery (COD), and UPI* are analyzed based on transaction frequency and revenue generated.

## Overall Sales

The dataset records total sales of approximately *28.22 million* across the 1,500 transactions.

The analysis shows that product performance varies significantly, with some products contributing a much larger share of overall sales than others.
